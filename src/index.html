03 / MGU-H + TURBOCHARGER

Give exhaust
a second purpose.

The turbine extracts work from exhaust gas. Its shaft-connected motor-generator converts some of that work into electricity, or drives the compressor to reduce turbo lag.

THE ENERGY PATHExhaust → shaft → electricity

Energy can go to the store or directly toward the MGU-K. This is shaft-power recovery, not direct heat-to-electric conversion.

04 / MGU-K

Braking energy.
Reimagined.

Coupled to the crankshaft, the MGU-K harvests energy during braking and adds torque during acceleration. Power flows both ways through the same machine.

2014–2025 ENERGY LIMITS120 kW maximum power

Up to 2 MJ per lap from MGU-K to store; up to 4 MJ from store to MGU-K. These are distinct energy paths.

05 / ENERGY STORE

The quiet part
of going fast.

The battery buffers recovered energy until the driver needs it. Control electronics balance deployment, charge and temperature constraints.

IDEALIZED FULL-POWER EQUIVALENT33.3 seconds

A simple energy-to-power ratio, excluding losses and direct MGU-H supply. It is not a total ERS operating-time limit.
